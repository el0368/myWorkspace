This video introduces the fourth limb of Ashtanga Yoga, **Pranayama**, which is the practice of controlling or regulating your life force through breath.

---

### Key Concepts of Pranayama

- **Meaning**: The word Pranayama is made of two parts: **"prana,"** which means life force, and **"yama,"** which means control or regulation. The practice involves using specific breathing techniques to gain control over this life force.
    
- **The Power of Breath**: The speaker emphasizes that your breath is the secret to the quality of your life, mind, and overall existence. The way you breathe directly influences your well-being.
    
- **Benefits**: The video lists several benefits of a regular pranayama practice, including:
    
    - Improved blood circulation
        
    - Enhanced sleeping patterns
        
    - Increased immunity
        
    - Improved metabolism
        
    - Better mental and emotional stability
        

The video concludes by reiterating that all aspects of yoga, including the physical poses (asanas) and breathing techniques, are interconnected and should be practiced together.
