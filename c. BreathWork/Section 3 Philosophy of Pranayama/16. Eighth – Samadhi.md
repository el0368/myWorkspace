The video describes **Samadhi** as the eighth and final limb of Ashtanga Yoga, representing the ultimate stage of yogic practice and the pinnacle of the experience. It is the state of **ultimate bliss** and **liberation**.

---

### The Meaning of Samadhi

The speaker breaks down the word **Samadhi** into its Sanskrit components: **"Sama"** (equanimity) and **"dhi"** (intellect). Samadhi, therefore, means having a balanced, equanimous intellect. It is the state where individual consciousness merges with universal consciousness, and a sense of "oneness with the divine" is experienced.

---

### The Path to Samadhi

The speaker emphasizes that the last three limbs of yoga—**Dharana (concentration), Dhyana (meditation), and Samadhi**—are often grouped together as **Samyama**. The practice of Samyama, or sustained and disciplined focus, is what leads to the profound state of Samadhi.

---

### The Importance of the Entire Process

A central theme of the video is that the goal is not just to reach Samadhi but to focus on the **entire process** of yoga. The speaker uses the analogy of a chair with four legs . Just as the chair collapses if even one leg is missing, your spiritual and personal growth will be incomplete if you neglect any of the eight limbs of yoga. You must practice them all simultaneously:

- **Yama and Niyama:** Taking care of your surroundings and your internal environment.
    
- **Asana:** Moving your body.
    
- **Pranayama:** Taking care of your breath.
    
- **Pratyahara, Dharana, and Dhyana:** Taking care of your mind through sensory withdrawal, concentration, and meditation.
    

The final destination of this journey is **Kaivalya**, or liberation, which is a state of complete freedom and enlightenment. However, the speaker stresses that true fulfillment comes from dedicating yourself to the full practice, not just the final goal.
