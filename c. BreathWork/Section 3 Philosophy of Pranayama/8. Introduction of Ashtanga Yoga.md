This video provides an introduction to **Ashtanga Yoga**, which is presented not as a style of yoga, but as a complete philosophical framework. The speaker explains that Ashtanga is based on the **Patanjali Yoga Sutras** and is also known as **Raja Yoga**.

---

### Understanding Ashtanga Yoga

- **Meaning**: The term "Ashtanga" is a Sanskrit word made of two parts: "ashta" (eight) and "anga" (limbs). Therefore, Ashtanga Yoga refers to the **eight limbs or stages of yoga**.
    
- **A Philosophical Framework**: The speaker emphasizes that Ashtanga is not just about physical sequences like the primary or secondary series. It's a foundational framework for your entire yoga practice that helps you build a structure to go deeper in your journey.
    
- **Holistic Benefits**: Practicing Ashtanga Yoga helps to awaken a deeper understanding of oneself, cultivate inner peace, and create a sense of balance. The speaker uses the analogy of a king ruling his kingdom, suggesting that by mastering your "inner kingdom" through Ashtanga, your outer life will fall into place.
    
- **Simultaneous Practice**: A crucial point made in the video is that the eight limbs are not meant to be practiced sequentially, like a step-by-step process. Instead, all eight limbs are interconnected and should be practiced **simultaneously**, like a "string of pearls" where each one is threaded together.
    

### The Eight Limbs of Yoga

The video briefly introduces the names of the eight limbs, which will be discussed in detail in future videos. They are:

1. **Yama**
    
2. **Niyama**
    
3. **Asana**
    
4. **Pranayama**
    
5. **Pratyahara**
    
6. **Dharana**
    
7. **Dhyana**
    
8. **Samadhi**
    

The speaker notes that the first two limbs, Yama and Niyama, each have five subcategories, which will be covered in subsequent videos.
