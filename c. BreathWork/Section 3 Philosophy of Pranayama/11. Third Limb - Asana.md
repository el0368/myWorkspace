This video discusses the third limb of Ashtanga Yoga, **Asana**. The speaker emphasizes that Asana is a very small, though widely recognized, part of the entire practice of yoga.

---

### Understanding Asana

- **More Than a Pose**: The speaker argues against the common misconception that yoga is all about performing difficult or "fancy" poses seen on social media. They state that yoga is a complete lifestyle and philosophy, with only three of the 195-196 sutras in the Patanjali Yoga Sutras dedicated to Asana.
    
- **Definition**: Asana is defined not just as a posture, but as a pose in which you are both **steady and comfortable**. The video quotes the sutra **"Sthira Sukham Asanam,"** which translates to "a pose is steady and comfortable." An asana is a posture where your breath is steady and you are physically and mentally at ease.
    
- **Effortlessness**: A key principle is that while you may use effort to get into a pose, you must remain **completely effortless** while holding it. If you are struggling, it's not truly an asana but just an exercise.
    
- **Overcoming Duality**: The practice of Asana helps you overcome the duality of the mind, bringing a sense of unity between your body and mind. The goal is to become one with yourself in the pose.
    

The speaker concludes by reiterating that the ability to perform a complex pose is irrelevant. What matters is taking care of your body and mind and embodying the principles of steadiness and comfort within the posture.
