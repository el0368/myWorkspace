**Prana Vidya** is the knowledge or wisdom of **prana**, the life force that keeps us alive. The video explains that different types of prana govern various bodily functions, such as blinking, digestion, nutrient circulation, and motion.

The concept of Prana Vidya is discussed extensively in ancient scriptures, particularly in the **Upanishads**, which are considered the philosophical end of the Vedas.

The speaker highlights several key points about Prana Vidya:

- **Prana and Well-being**: When a person experiences stress, anxiety, or depression, it indicates that their prana has been depleted. Practices like yoga, meditation, and pranayama are considered "life savers" that help to uplift and restore this vital energy.
    
- **Beyond the Gross Body**: Practicing Prana Vidya allows an individual to transcend their physical existence and access deeper, more subtle layers of their being. This is achieved by linking all functions to the breath, which is the key to understanding prana.
    
- **Heightened Awareness**: A person who practices the knowledge of prana becomes more aware of their internal state. This awareness helps them go beyond the limitations of the five senses.
    
- **Holistic Transformation**: By focusing on Prana Vidya, one can create positive transformations in their life, as the ancient texts detail how prana enters, leaves, and divides to power various bodily functions. The video concludes by emphasizing that the responsibility of a yoga teacher is to preserve and share this authentic knowledge.
