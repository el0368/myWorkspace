The fourth key of Prana Vidya is the **psychic centers**, also known as **chakras**. These are subtle energy points in the body that hold prana (life force). They are located along the **Sushumna Nadi**, the central psychic passage.

### Understanding the Psychic Centers (Chakras)

- **Prana's Influence**: The speaker explains that prana can take different forms within the body, and these forms directly influence the chakras. By practicing Prana Vidya, you are actively trying to **stimulate and activate** these energy centers.
    
- **Interdependence**: The chakras are **interdependent**. It is not possible to focus on just one chakra in isolation, as activating one will inevitably impact the others. A holistic approach is therefore necessary for a balanced practice.
    
- **Significance**: Each chakra has its own unique significance and characteristics. The video suggests that understanding these characteristics is essential for a proper practice of Prana Vidya.
