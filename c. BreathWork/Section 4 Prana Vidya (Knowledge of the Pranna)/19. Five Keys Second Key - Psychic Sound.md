The second key to Prana Vidya is the **psychic sound**. The speaker explains that in addition to the external sounds we hear every day, there is an inner, subtle sound that can be perceived through focused practice.

---

### The Nature of Psychic Sound

- **Inner vs. Outer Sound:** The video distinguishes between sounds from the external environment and sounds that manifest "right within us." To hear these inner sounds, you must withdraw your auditory senses from external noises, which is a form of Pratyahara (withdrawal of the senses).
    
- **Sounds of the Universe:** Through practices like meditation and pranayama, you can begin to hear various psychic sounds, such as whistles, bells, sirens, or music like drums and flutes. The speaker notes that these sounds are not physically present and are considered to be the "sounds of the universe."
    
- **The Sound of Om:** As a practitioner's awareness deepens, the intensity of these sounds increases. The ultimate sound one can experience is the transcendental sound of **Om**, which is considered the universal sound.
    

The video concludes by stating that experiencing these psychic sounds is a step toward a deeper connection with the universe.
