The fifth and final key to Prana Vidya is the **psychic symbol**. This practice involves using a symbol, image, or object as a point of concentration to experience the energy within your body.

### The Role of Psychic Symbols

- **Tools for Concentration:** Psychic symbols are not random; they are tools for focusing your mind. Examples include a **yantra** (a geometric symbol), a **mandala**, or natural elements like a mountain, a cloud, or fire.
    
- **Accessing Internal Energy:** By concentrating on these symbols, you can perceive the subtle vibrations and energy within your own body. This practice is believed to influence your internal state and lead to a deeper awareness of your own bio-energy.
    
- **Personalized Nature:** Psychic symbols are often chosen based on an individual's personality, astrological sign, or are given to them during a spiritual initiation. This personalized approach makes the symbol more effective for a particular person's practice.
