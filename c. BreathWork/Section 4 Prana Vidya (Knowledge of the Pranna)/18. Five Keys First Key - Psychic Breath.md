The first of the "five keys of Prana Vidya" is **psychic breathing**, which is the practice of becoming consciously aware of your breath. The video explains that this awareness allows you to experience your **bioplasmic body**, also known as your aura or bio-vibes.

---

### Psychic Breathing vs. Normal Breathing

The speaker distinguishes psychic breathing from normal, unconscious breathing.

- **Normal Breathing:** This is the act of breathing in and out, whether it's the automatic, unconscious process or the conscious, effortful practice of pranayama.
    
- **Psychic Breathing:** This is a step beyond normal breathing. It's not about forcing the breath, but about bringing a heightened **awareness** to it. By focusing your awareness on the sensation of air moving through your nostrils and into your lungs, you begin to perceive the bioplasmic body.
    

The video uses the example of a deep sound in the throat during breathing, stating that by simply bringing awareness to this sound, you can transcend the physical body and access a deeper, more subtle experience of your bio-energy. This practice is presented as the initial step toward perceiving the auras and energies of yourself and others.
