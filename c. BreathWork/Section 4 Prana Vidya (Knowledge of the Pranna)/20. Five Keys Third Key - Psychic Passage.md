The third key to Prana Vidya is the **psychic passage**, which is an imagined or energetic channel in the body through which you direct your awareness, breath, and prana (life force) in a single stream. The goal is to purify the body from within by transferring prana to these passages.

---

### How Psychic Passages Work

- **Energetic Channels**: A psychic passage is not a physical tube; it's an energetic channel that can be created in any part of the body. By focusing your psychic breath (conscious awareness of breath) on this passage, you stimulate it and transfer prana to it.
    
- **Purification**: The process of directing prana through these passages is said to purify the entire body. It’s a method for cleaning and energizing specific areas by concentrating your life force there.
    

---

### Key Examples of Psychic Passages

The video highlights several well-known psychic passages that correspond to the body's energy centers (chakras):

- **The Frontal Passage**: A passage that runs from the **Manipura Chakra** (solar plexus) to the **Vishuddhi Chakra** (throat chakra).
    
- **Another Psychic Passage**: A passage that runs from the **Mooladhara Chakra** (root chakra) to the **Ajna Chakra** (eyebrow chakra).
    
- **The Most Powerful Passage**: The **Sushumna Nadi**, which is the central energy channel that runs from the **Mooladhara Chakra** to the **Sahasrara Chakra** (crown chakra).
    

By working with these passages, you are in a way stimulating the trigger points that activate the chakras, which are all part of the body's "prana sharira" or energetic body.
