This video guides viewers through the breathing exercise **Kumbhaka**, a specific pranayama technique, followed by a guided meditation. Here is a detailed breakdown of the practice without using the specific term.

### The Breathing Exercise

This part of the practice is based on a **1:1:1 ratio**, with each phase lasting for a count of **four**.

1. **Preparation**: Begin by sitting comfortably with your legs crossed and your spine straight. Your hands should be in a specific position where your thumb and index finger touch on each knee. Close your eyes, relax your shoulders, and keep your head level. Take a few deep, normal breaths to relax your mind and body.
    
2. **Inhalation**: Breathe in slowly and deeply through your nose for a count of four.
    
3. **Retention**: After inhaling, hold the breath inside your body for a count of four.
    
4. **Exhalation**: Slowly breathe out through your nose for a count of four.
    
5. **Repetition**: Repeat this three-part cycle for several rounds, maintaining a steady rhythm. The goal is to feel your chest expand and contract with each breath.
    

---

### The Guided Meditation

Once the breathing exercise is finished, you will immediately transition into a meditation while keeping your eyes closed.

1. **Transition**: Change your hand position by turning your palms upward to face the sky. Continue to breathe normally.
    
2. **Grounding**: Take a long, deep breath, imagining that you are breathing with every part of your body. As you exhale, release all tension and effort.
    
3. **Visualization**: Imagine your body is inside a tall pillar of energy or a beam of light that stretches from the floor to the ceiling.
    
4. **Mindful Awareness**: Pay attention to your breath's movement. With every breath in, direct your focus upward toward the ceiling. With every breath out, move your focus downward toward the floor.
    
5. **Purification**: As you continue to focus on this upward and downward movement, visualize any feelings of restlessness or emotional blockages being "burned away" by the light within the pillar.
    
6. **Embracing Feelings**: If you feel any physical or mental restlessness, simply become aware of it and accept it without judgment.
    
7. **Conscious Expansion**: As your breathing continues, your mind becomes quieter, and your awareness expands.
    
8. **Conclusion**: Take a final deep breath, let go of any remaining effort, and then slowly and gently open your eyes when you feel ready.
