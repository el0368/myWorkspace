### Why the Linked List is the Next Step

1. **It's the Next Topic on the Roadmap:** The visual layout of the **Data Structures** section places "Linked List" immediately after "Array"2222.
    
2. **It's the Fundamental Alternative:** Arrays and Linked Lists are the two most fundamental ways to store a sequence of data. Learning about linked lists provides a crucial contrast to what you just learned about arrays. You will explore a completely different approach to the same problem, which will deepen your understanding of data structure trade-offs.
    

You will learn:

- How linked lists store data in non-contiguous memory using nodes and pointers.
    
- Why they are slow for accessing elements (**O(n)**) but very fast for inserting and deleting elements (**O(1)**).
    

Mastering both arrays and linked lists provides the foundation you'll need for more complex data structures like

**Stacks** 3and

**Queues**4, which are often implemented using one of these two structures.
